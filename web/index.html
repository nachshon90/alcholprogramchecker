Check one label

Type in your company and product details, add a picture of the label, and press Check this label.

Everything happens inside your web browser. Your label pictures and company details are never uploaded anywhere. There is no server doing the checking – the text reading runs on this device.

You can send one or two pictures. Many containers put the brand on the front and the health warning on the back, so if your product has two labels, send both and they will be checked together as one container. If everything is on a single label, just send one.
